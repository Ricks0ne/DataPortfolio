Bicycle Commuting Research

Determinants of Students' Adoption of Bicycle Commuting

Overview

This research examined the key determinants influencing students’ adoption of bicycle commuting at the University of Lagos. Using SPSS regression models, the study revealed how factors such as distance, safety perception, and gender affect willingness to cycle.

Tools Used

  • SPSS
  • Excel
  • Regression Analysis

Findings

• Distance and perceived safety were the strongest predictors of bicycle use.
• Proposed on-campus infrastructure improvements to support sustainable mobility.
